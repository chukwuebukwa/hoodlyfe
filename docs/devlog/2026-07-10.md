## 04:31 - Production-Reference Vehicle Damage Deepening

### Why This Replaced the First Balance Pass

The initial vehicle checkpoint correctly wired collision, weapon damage, destruction, and presentation, but its 100-point health scale was a shallow prototype. The GTA III/Vice City reverse-engineered source shows a much deeper production model: 1000 starting health, impulse and handling-based collision damage, localized body components, staged engine damage, collision-to-fire clamping, and a five-second burning window before explosion.

### Research

- Inspected `daynz/GTAviceCity` at commit `3233ffe1c4b99e8efb4c41c6794b4fce880cf503`.
- Traced vehicle initialization and weapon damage in `Vehicle.cpp`.
- Traced collision thresholds, per-part damage, fire state, particles, and explosion timing in `Automobile.cpp`.
- Traced engine, wheel, door, panel, light, bonnet, boot, bumper, and windscreen progression in `DamageManager.h/.cpp`.
- Recorded the behavior map, legal boundary, multiplayer adaptations, and calibration targets in `docs/VEHICLE_DAMAGE_RESEARCH.md`.
- Added ADR 0003 making production-reference research a required gate for every substantial gameplay domain.

### Delivered

- Raised base vehicle durability from 100 to 1000 and replicated explicit maximum health.
- Increased significant collision damage while keeping ordinary traffic contacts minor relative to the larger pool.
- Made configured weapon damage apply directly to vehicles instead of using the placeholder reduction.
- Added front, rear, left, and right accumulated body damage based on impact direction.
- Added independent engine damage stages at 100, 150, 200, and on-fire at 225.
- Changed handling degradation to follow engine state rather than linearly penalizing every point of body damage.
- Changed collision/wall lethality to clamp at one health, ignite, telegraph danger, and explode after a server-owned five-second fuse.
- Kept direct weapon lethality immediate when damage fully exhausts health.
- Added typed ignition events and source attribution across the fire fuse.
- Extended smoke, fire, condition HUD, and debug labels to use engine, fire, maximum-health, and localized damage state.

### QA
