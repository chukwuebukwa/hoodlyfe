## 00:34 CST - G1 Shared Crime, Wanted, and Police Response Budget

- Compared pinned re3 and reVC wanted, pursuit-slot, cop, population, vehicle, and car-control
  implementations before changing ownership. The transferable production behavior is a
  bounded response budget, explicit unit registration/release, materially better unit
  replacement, and police population admission separate from wanted pressure. The reversed
  sources remain read-only educational references; the TypeScript policy and tuning are
  original NOCK0 work.
- Replaced separate foot and cruiser dispatch owners with one modular,
  server-authoritative `PoliceResponseAllocationSystem`. It allocates a maximum five foot
  units and three cruisers under an 11-point district budget, gives simultaneous suspects
  deterministic weighted shares, retains leases, replaces poor assignments only after
  distance and time hysteresis, and releases hard-limit surplus immediately when heat falls.
- Made report identity part of allocation. Expired foot/cruiser searches suppress only the
  exact unit, suspect, and report revision; a newer witness report clears suppression. Death,
  suspect cleanup, unavailable officers, destroyed or hijacked cruisers, and player-driven
  police cars release shared leases without leaving a second private assignment map.
- Kept responsibilities separate: `CrimeResponseController` owns reports and the room-facing
  facade, the allocator owns finite leases, `PursuitMemory` owns last-known search state,
  foot/cruiser controllers execute behavior, and `PoliceResponseFleetController` realizes
  aggregate cruiser demand. `DistrictRoom` only wires ports and diagnostics.

  maximum points, foot/cruiser occupancy and caps, demanded suspects, and suppressed pairs;
  snapshots deep-copy demand, assignment, and change records.
- Hardened the real two-client scenario for the now-active systemic world. Fire retries are
  bounded by actor action state when police reactions interrupt commands, authoritative
  kill/incident/pursuit evidence replaces frozen-world assumptions, and mission-contact
  routing periodically replans and ranks complete paths by current vehicle conflicts.
- Audited the milestone against the frozen netcode contract. No prediction, reconciliation,
  remote timeline, AOI, interaction-island, rewind, rollout, or shared movement/contact
  implementation file changed. AI, wanted, allocation, search, fleet, damage, and economy
  remain server-only and never execute during replay.

## 15:57 CST - G2b.1 Junction Conflict-Zone Ownership

- Compared pinned re3 and reVC traffic-light annotation, directional stop-window,
  traffic-speed, route-curve, and legitimate-stop cleanup behavior before changing
  junction ownership. The transferable behavior is authored path control, stopping only
  before the line, continuing after commitment, and keeping policy separate from physical
  movement. NOCK0 adds an original server-side ownership layer because multiplayer players,
  pedestrians, and vehicles can physically occupy the same conflict zone.
- Replaced the fixed boolean lock with a modular deterministic lifecycle: `waiting`,
  `approach`, `crossing`, and `clearing`. FIFO queue order is stable, active leases renew,
  blocked approach owners relinquish admission without losing queue position, committed
  cars continue, and ownership remains until the rear collider clears the outgoing node.
  Lease expiry is now abandoned-owner recovery rather than the normal release path.
- Bound admission to the exact centers of the twelve authored junctions. Relevant signals
  and unqueued physical occupants block new entry, while a 34-pixel virtual stop obstacle
  keeps denied cars before the connector. Authored routes start admission at 112 pixels and
  preserve a 60-pixel commit window; the broad legacy road-cell adapter intentionally does
  not infer physical conflict centers.
- Protected crossing/clearing phases suppress reverse, pass, merge, and siren-yield
  maneuvers. Hijacking, route recovery, release, and route changes drain stale queue state.
  `TrafficController` remains the composition boundary; route ownership, junction policy,
