## Open Elevated Edges

- Preserved GTA2 side-face data as per-surface barriers instead of flattening it into the district collision grid.
- Changed the surface physics compiler to distinguish internal walls from exposed elevated edges. A side face remains solid when both sides are on the same elevation or no lower floor exists; it is omitted when crossing it has a valid lower landing surface.
- This lets vehicles and pedestrians leave open bridge, roof, and platform edges through the shared airborne simulation instead of being stopped by an invisible two-dimensional wall.
- Kept authored internal walls in Rapier so the change does not make same-level partitions permeable.
- Accounted for GTA2 side faces that are authored one block inward from the geometric ledge. The compiler now probes both wall normals and opens the wall only when one side reaches a lower drivable sheet within a block while the opposite side remains elevated.
- Added generated-map coverage for the tall Industrial District bridge instead of relying only on synthetic surface fixtures.
