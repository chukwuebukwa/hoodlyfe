# Development Log - 2026-07-19

## 00:41 CDT - Complete GTA2 World and Three.js Geometry Streaming

- Expanded the active `bil` level from the staged `96 x 96` crop to the complete
  `256 x 256` GTA2 source map. The playable world is now `16384 x 16384` pixels and
  includes exported visuals, static collision, road classification, pedestrian surfaces,
  height data, and Three geometry for all 65,536 source tiles.
- Replaced the full-world Three monolith with a versioned manifest and 1,024 independently
  loadable `8 x 8`-tile chunks. The shared atlas and compact manifest stay resident; visible
  chunks load first, one ring preloads, two rings retain, velocity requests geometry ahead,
  and distant meshes unload with GPU geometry disposal.
- Added bounded request ownership: four concurrent chunk fetches, cancellation outside the
  desired set, deterministic visible/preload priority, failure accounting, payload-coordinate
  validation, and initial visible-chunk priming before the first rendered frame.
- Added F3 and in-world streaming diagnostics for loaded, loading, queued, retained, failed,
  and loaded-triangle pressure. The full 45 MB chunk set is available on disk but ordinary
  play keeps only a local camera-interest subset in Three.js.
- Kept static collision resident intentionally. The full occupancy grid is small, avoids
  collision pop-in, and gives local movement/vehicle prediction immediate deterministic map
  queries. Dynamic population remains a separate server AOI system with bounded hot actors
  and lightweight virtual records.
- Scaled the potential virtual population pool with source-map area, capped at eight times
  the 96-tile reference density. The complete world owns 512 civilian, 57 police, and 455
  traffic records, while active authoritative caps remain 40 pedestrians and 24 vehicles.
- Preserved the stable `(96,97)` authoring reference. Moving the active source origin from
  `(80,81)` to `(0,0)` rebased gameplay by `+5120,+5184` pixels; spawn, lanes, interiors,
  doors, fixtures, service anchors, signals, lights, and population zones keep their exact
  relative geography. Mercy Hospital remains `+552,-136` pixels from spawn.
- Fixed chunk-origin floating-point drift in roof selection. A `113.9999999` boundary value
  had omitted six Southside Clinic triangles; epsilon-tolerant bounds restore the original
  roof contracts at `32 / 24 / 42 / 48` triangles without changing authored definitions.
- Made `map:expand` install complete chunk directories transactionally and remove the legacy
  monolith when a full-world export does not produce one. Expanded structural validation to
  cover manifest/frame parity, complete chunk coverage, unique coordinates, files, triangle
  totals, surface dimensions, interiors, lane topology, and all authored gameplay anchors.
- Reworked the map and interior guides around full-world source coordinates, streamed
  presentation geometry, resident deterministic collision, and the remaining content work:
  the central lane graph and handcrafted services are translated correctly but do not yet
  author production traffic and activities across every outer district.

QA: map validation passes for 1,024 chunks and 344,523 source triangles. Focused map,
interior, population, and streaming coverage passes 22/22. The optimized Next production
build and TypeScript pass. The complete suite passes 560 checks; its existing long-form
multiplayer scenario still times out at the late mission-hostile melee replication wait in
