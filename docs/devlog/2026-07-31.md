# 2026-07-31

## Tall GTA2 Bridge Underpass Import

- Reproduced the blocked lower passage at the `qa=bridge` fixture near world position
  `(3936, 2464)`. The exporter preserved the bridge deck but omitted the lower street
  sheet through the center of the structure, causing surface-aware collision to treat
  the bridge footprint as unavailable at ground level.
- Confirmed this is a GTA2 column-encoding case rather than a runtime Z-order problem.
  The visible deck is stored at `z=6`; interior columns begin at `z=5`, leaving the
  lower street volume empty, while edge and support columns begin at `z=2`.
- Extended the exporter to retain each column's offset and height and reconstruct an
  implicit lower sheet only when a bounded gap has one consistent elevated cover and
  its interior columns are empty below that cover. Edge supports remain allowed, but
  solid support rows do not receive a lower sheet.
- Replaced the earlier short-span bridge heuristic with a row/column linear scan. This
  supports tall and long encoded structures without a fixed bridge-length cap or a
  quadratic search over candidate endpoints.
- Regenerated the complete 256 x 256 surface manifest. At the reported bridge, the
  center passage now contains a connected lower sheet at world elevation `128` and an
  independent deck at `384`; neighboring solid support rows remain deck-only.
- Added a generated-world regression covering the tall bridge and retained the older
  `z=192` underpass regression. The focused surface, physics, and airborne suite passes
  25/25 tests, and `npm run build` passes.
- Browser QA on the isolated bridge worktree at
  `http://127.0.0.1:5175/?renderer=three&qa=bridge` loads the Three renderer and authored
  surface scene without runtime errors. The only browser warning is the existing
  `THREE.Clock` deprecation.
- The complete repository suite currently reports five failures in population,
  multiplayer mission timing, population clock expectations, and traffic soak tests.
  Those are outside the bridge importer scope and were not masked or rewritten during
  this fix.
